## 9. Conclusion and Recommendations

**Key Insight**: The Hongdae entrance commercial area has top-tier foot traffic but is highly saturated with cafes, so survival is difficult as an "ordinary cafe." You must intensively target women in their 20s with strong photo zone / SNS virality points and signature menus.

**Short-term Action Items** (before launch):
- [ ] On-site visits to actual properties (compare at least 5 properties)
- [ ] When negotiating rent, request "minimizing deposit ratio" terms
- [ ] Visit 2-3 competitor cafes weekly to benchmark menu and operations

**Mid-term Action Items** (3 months after opening):
- [ ] Instagram account 5,000 follower goal (induce photo zone posting)
- [ ] Introduce regular customer membership program (target 30% repeat visit rate)
- [ ] Target 200 monthly orders on Baemin (Baedal Minjok)
